# Photography shot list

The site uses only real Northminster photos, taken from the old website. Many are phone photos from 2018–2021. A single Sunday plus one Wednesday night with a volunteer photographer would lift the whole site.

**Guidance:** use natural light. Capture people *doing* things, not posing. Shoot horizontally **and** vertically, because phones use vertical crops. Upload originals at 2000px+ wide. Get consent from families before publishing photos of children.

## Highest priority

1. **Hero, Sunday worship (wide and vertical):** the congregation singing, with the stained glass and the tented ceiling visible. Replaces `sanctuary-scripture-reading.jpg` and `sanctuary-congregation.jpg`.
2. **Arriving:** someone being greeted at the door, and coffee and donuts on the patio. There is no current photo of what a visitor sees first.
3. **The outside of the campus, in daylight:** the sanctuary roof from Clairemont Mesa Blvd, the entrance, and **the parking lot**. These go on the Visit page. The current exterior photo is only 1044px wide.
4. **Sunday School in action:** kids with their teacher in the Youth room.
5. **Youth Group (grades 6–12):** Sunday night dinner and conversation. The Students page currently shows placeholders.
6. **Wednesday Family Night:** people at the dinner table, and adults in Bible study.
